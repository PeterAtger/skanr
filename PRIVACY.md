# skanr privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

skanr is a document scanner with no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

## What stays on your phone

Your scans, their names and the text read from them are stored in skanr's private storage on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. They are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall skanr.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include skanr's files in your phone's backup to your Google account, the same as for other apps. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

## Scanning

skanr uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, which is part of Google Play services. Google states that the scanner processes images on your phone and does not send them to Google's servers. Because the scanner has its own camera access, skanr does not ask for camera permission.

## Reading text

To suggest names, power search and make searchable PDFs, skanr uses Google's ML Kit text recognition, which is built into the app and runs on your phone. Your images and the text found in them are not sent anywhere.

## Data Google's libraries send

Google's ML Kit libraries send Google diagnostic and usage information, such as device model, app information and how well the scanning and text features perform. This does not include your images or their text. Google describes this in the [ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and [ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure). skanr itself makes no network requests. The internet permission in the app is there for these Google libraries.

## Sharing

A scan leaves skanr only when you tap Share or Save to phone. It goes to the app or folder you choose, and what happens there is covered by that app's own policy.

## Children

skanr does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
