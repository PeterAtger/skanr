# skanr privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

skanr is a document scanner with no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It has no internet access at all. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

## What stays on your phone

Your scans, their names and the text read from them are stored in skanr's private storage on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. They are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall skanr.

skanr opts out of Android cloud backup, so your scans are never copied to your Google account. A direct phone-to-phone transfer when you set up a new phone may still bring them along, since that never goes through the cloud.

## Scanning

skanr uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, which is part of Google Play services and runs as a separate system component. Google states that the scanner processes images on your phone and does not send them to Google's servers. Google Play services is covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Because the scanner has its own camera access, skanr does not ask for camera permission.

## Reading text

To suggest names, power search and make searchable PDFs, skanr uses Google's ML Kit text recognition, which is built into the app and runs on your phone. Your images and the text found in them are not sent anywhere.

## No internet access

Google's ML Kit libraries normally send Google usage reports, such as device model and how well text reading performs ([details](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure)). skanr removes the internet permission from the app, so neither skanr nor these libraries can send anything.

## Sharing

A scan leaves skanr only when you tap Share or Save to phone. It goes to the app or folder you choose, and what happens there is covered by that app's own policy.

## Children

skanr does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
