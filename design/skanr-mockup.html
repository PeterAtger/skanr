skanr

A document scanner with nothing in the way. No ads, no account, no upgrade screens. Scans stay on the phone until you share them. This is the whole first-run flow, from opening the app to sending a PDF.

What's left out on purpose

  • No cloud sync or accounts. Files live in the app's own storage. Backing up is a share away.
  • No camera permission. Google's scanner uses its own, so skanr never asks for it.
  • No greeting or tips on the home screen. The welcome message shows once, on first open, and the list takes over after that.
